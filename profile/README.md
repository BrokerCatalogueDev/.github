# BrokerCatalogue

**Compare forex brokers, CFD brokers and prop firms.** [brokercatalogue.com](https://brokercatalogue.com/)

BrokerCatalogue helps traders pick a broker or a prop firm with the facts in front of them. We compare forex and CFD brokers on regulation, costs, platforms and account types. We also track prop trading firms: their rules, fees, payout terms and status.

## Open data

**Prop firm rules, fees and regulator log.** The rules, fees and payout terms of prop trading firms, each with sources and a confidence level. Plus a dated log of what regulators have said about prop firms. Free to use under CC BY 4.0.

- Live page with the newest data: https://brokercatalogue.com/prop-firm-data/
- Fixed copy of version 1: [`prop-firm-data`](https://github.com/BrokerCatalogueDev/prop-firm-data)

Please credit BrokerCatalogue and link to the live page when you use the data. Found a mistake? Email info@brokercatalogue.com with a link to the source.
